- **#13 Schema v7 delta** — **answered 2026-08-18.** v7 is the SkyTrax format, and murmelbahn upstream now documents it (`8d7974a`, `lib/src/app/skytrax.rs`). The mystery `u32 = 13` is `CourseElementGeneration::SkyTrax` — the generation *field*, relocated from its v4 position to just after metadata; "13" was its value in the fixtures, not a constant. The rest of the delta: layers store `(id, kind, position, integer small-stacker height)`; tiles carry both power and light-stone fields; a vertex-connector array and a 32-byte sha256 tail close the file (decomposition byte-verified on both oracle fixtures). - **#14 Why four of six adjacent directions have no connecting rotation** — **answered 2026-08-08.** The suspect was right: the starter's `hex_rotation` was pinned at 0 for all 42 cells of the 2026-08-07 sweep, and it moves which directions connect.
