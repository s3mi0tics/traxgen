# s52-t17 — which GOAL_BASIN rotations catch a marble, and does it finish? (order step 2: the first finishing run on another goal piece)

**Status:** OPEN

## Open (written before the work)

- **Goal:** one standard-square board with six launch pads, each with a GOAL_BASIN on its East side at rotation 0 to 5, played once; read per pad whether a marble loaded and where its trail ends, and whether the app's finish burst fires.
- **Scope:** `scripts/probe_goal_basin.py` (new), `tests/test_probe_goal_basin.py` (new).
- **Budget:** 35 minutes, one upload, up to two emulator boots.
- **Done check:**
  1. *Step:* build the board, test it offline, upload, play by link (`probe_finish_frames --link`), read trails (`read_trail_ends`) and the finish.
  2. *True after:* for each rotation, marble loaded or not, and trail end to the basin in pixels; the finish verdict for the board.
  3. *Miss cases:* a pad's trail is attributed to a neighbour (pads too close); a run on a non-course screen is read (the load check refuses those, D102); a pad hidden under the toolbar.
  4. *Reads:* the run's frames and `frames.json`; the manifest the script writes.
  5. *Check:* the report lists all six rotations with loaded/not and trail distance, and the outcomes declared below are scored against them.
- **Outcomes declared before any live run:** marble travelling East enters the basin by world side 3, piece side `(3 - r) % 6`; the corpus (s52-t13) puts the basin's rail entrances on piece sides 0, 2, 4. So rotations **1, 3, 5 catch** (trail ends in the basin) and rotations **0, 2, 4 have no valid connection, so no marble loads on those pads** (s44). The board finishes (sparkle burst) if at least one rotation catches and the basin bursts like a goal rail. Any other pattern means the basin's entrances are not where the corpus's rails attach, which is itself a finding.
